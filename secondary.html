The day

One experience. Three ways to book.

01

PE Takeover

We take over your scheduled PE lessons for the day, with each class enjoying its own team golf session.

Ideal for adding variety to your PE programme with minimal timetable disruption.

02

The Year Group Challenge

Choose the year groups you'd like to involve, and we'll organise the experience around their sessions.

Ideal for Year 7 transition, KS3 enrichment, curriculum variety or targeted participation initiatives.

03

The All Day Challenge

One selected group of around 30 students enjoys an extended golf experience, with even more games, skill development, team challenges and competition.

Ideal for enrichment groups, reward days and sports development.

The National Putting Challenge

Beat the nation.

10 shots. Your school. The national leaderboard. Every school gets the chance to put its name on the national leaderboard through a lunchtime putting challenge.

  • 10 scoring shotsYour school takes 10 shots in the challenge, with points awarded by the agreed scoring system.
  • Choose your teamPick your 10 strongest putters, or use the challenge to engage pupils who might not usually take part in sport.
  • Represent your schoolPupils contribute to their school's official challenge score.
  • Join the national leaderboardYour score is submitted so your school competes against other schools throughout the year.
