About Stu

A coach on a mission to grow the game.

Stu Warren is a golf coach who believes golf should be for everyone. He works with schools, juniors, club golfers and professionals, and his coaching follows three simple ideas: enhance your game, enjoy it, and excel.

His biggest passion is introducing young people to golf. Through his school programmes he has put clubs in the hands of thousands of children across the UK, giving many of them their first taste of the game.
