How it works

Every pupil counts.

Primary: during a Primary Golf Experience day, KS1 and KS2 pupils take part in the Hole-in-One Challenge in every game. Their results are combined to create your school's score.

Secondary: during First Swing, your school takes 10 scoring shots in a lunchtime putting challenge. Pick your best putters, or use it to involve pupils who don't usually play sport.
