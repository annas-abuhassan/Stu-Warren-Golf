The big idea

One day. The whole school. Everyone gets to play.

The Primary Golf Experience takes the excitement of golf directly into school, creating an active, inclusive and memorable enrichment day for every pupil from Reception to Year 6.

Using safe, age-appropriate equipment and fast-paced games, pupils discover golf through movement, teamwork, challenges and competition. No previous golf experience is needed. You bring the school, we'll bring the golf.

  • Every pupilReception to Year 6. Every child gets the chance to pick up a club and have a go.
  • EverywherePlayground, field, MUGA or sports hall. Indoors or outdoors.
  • EveryoneAccessible, active and engaging for children of different ages and abilities, with SEND-friendly games and equipment.

The experience

One school. Three golf experiences.

Every age gets a different challenge, and the whole school shares one experience.

EYFS + KS1

Roll in the Hole

Aim. Strike. Run. Score.

Children use easy-to-handle clubs and large sponge balls to explore movement and accuracy while racing around a dynamic putting course.

Years 3 + 4

Chipping Fun

Precision. Strategy. Teamwork.

Fast-paced chipping action where teams attack targets and flags, developing coordination, accuracy and game sense.

Years 5 + 6

Driver Smash

Power. Balance. Competition.

A high-energy driving challenge where teams compete to hit targets and score points.

The National Hole-in-One Challenge

Can your school beat the rest of England?

Every school taking part competes in the National Hole-in-One Challenge. Throughout the day, every pupil has the chance to score holes in one in every game. Together these make your school's score, which goes onto our national leaderboard.

  • Take partEvery pupil gets an opportunity to have a go.
  • ScoreSuccessful attempts count towards your school's result.
  • SubmitYour school's score enters the national competition.
  • ClimbWatch your school move up the national leaderboard.
